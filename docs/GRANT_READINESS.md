# VOX and the .tech No Excuses Fund

Reviewed October 21, 2026 against the [fund](https://noexcuses.get.tech/) and [official terms](https://noexcuses.get.tech/terms). This is a preparation document, not a submitted application or a prediction of an award.

The programme considers a working product, technical execution, problem insight, evidence of use, potential, originality and credibility. Its current deadline is December 15, 2026, with deadlines stated in US Pacific Time. Its .tech requirement covers the functioning product and primary public website, not a redirect or a standalone landing page. The operator determines eligibility. No terms have been accepted or application submitted as part of this work.

## Product thesis

VOX is an operations co-worker for independent maintainers and small teams. A voice or text goal becomes a constrained plan, safe saved work and a handoff of the decisions that still require a person. Support operations is the first working workflow, as specified in the PRD. A broader product positioning does not imply that arbitrary customer applications or mailboxes are connected.

## What a reviewer can use

- [getvox.tech/judge](https://getvox.tech/judge): isolated fictional helpdesk, individual approvals, saved history and evidence export.
- Live AI evaluation subject to the existing eight-pass ceiling. Rules-only exploration remains available without model calls. Neither mode sends customer messages.
- Three editable workflow starters: full queue preparation, urgent work, and a queue briefing. A shortcut grants no authority; the generated plan still needs approval.
- A returning user's handoff points to pending decisions, interrupted work or the saved outcome. Counts come from that run.
- Local Gemma through Ollama uses the same execution boundary and explicitly skips cloud tools. [Measured run](evidence/lazy-payoff.json), [ten-run timing](evidence/local-performance.json), [Tinker evaluation](evidence/tinker-evaluation.json).

## Evidence still needed for a stronger startup case

| Question                             | Current evidence                                      | Next validation                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who needs this enough to adopt it?   | PRD persona and a working synthetic support journey   | Interviews with maintainers/support leads; permission to quote their findings                       |
| Does it reduce total human effort?   | Local preparation timings; no timed manual baseline   | Run the [manual timing protocol](LAZY_PAYOFF.md), including review and corrections                  |
| Do people return?                    | Technical verification only                           | Consented pilot use and honest activation/retention measurements                                    |
| Can it operate on real support work? | Fictional helpdesk with simulated sends               | A separately scoped, read-only customer connector and privacy/ownership review before any real data |
| Is it a commercial service?          | Working open-source preview; no paid plan or checkout | Validate a pricing hypothesis with users before enabling billing                                    |

There are no claims of customers, revenue, production SLAs, measured minutes saved or grant endorsement. A polished preview strengthens assessability; it does not replace usage evidence. Real multi-tenant ingestion, production helpdesk access and sending remain outside the current PRD's MVP scope.
