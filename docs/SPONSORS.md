## Hosting verification

The earlier Singapore deployment repeatedly exceeded Temporal's three-second task-dispatch budget against Tiger in us-east-1. The approved free Virginia replacement passed the real startup guard, including activity retry and an approval signal. Its TLS-verified driver probes reported a one-millisecond median. The recorded-voice workflow then survived a complete service suspend/resume with the same execution and no repeated completed provider work, before completing after the individual decisions.

The owner-approved cleanup backed up and deleted only the suspended Murf-ai-2 web service to free the hosting slot. Its repository and external databases were preserved. The old VOX Singapore service was suspended after the domain moved; its Atlas ranges were removed. No Supabase project, paid Temporal Cloud service or new database was created for this migration.
